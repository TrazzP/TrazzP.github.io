FAQ

Questions about this clinic.

Do I need to live in Bozeman to join?

No, but you'll get the most value if this is your primary clinic for in-person visits. We also offer telemedicine for members who travel.

What services are available at this location?

Our Bozeman clinic offers primary care, pharmacy, and dental services. All three are available to members today.

Can I switch to a different Onevia location later?

Yes. Your membership is portable across all Onevia locations. Just update your primary clinic in your member portal.

Is there wheelchair access?

Yes. Our clinic is fully ADA accessible with wheelchair-friendly entrances and exam rooms.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Members are welcome to walk in during open hours, though we recommend scheduling for shorter wait times.

What if I need care after hours?

Members have 24/7 access to our on-call physician via text or phone for urgent questions and guidance.
