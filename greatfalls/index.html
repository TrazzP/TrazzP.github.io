FAQ

Questions about Great Falls.

When exactly will Great Falls open?

We're targeting 2026 but don't have a specific date yet. Join the waitlist for updates as we finalize our location and hiring.

Can I join Onevia now at an existing clinic?

Yes! Our Missoula clinic is open and accepting new members. Your membership will be portable to Great Falls when we open.

Will Great Falls pricing be the same as other locations?

Yes. Membership pricing is consistent across all Onevia locations.

What services will be available at launch?

Primary care will be available from day one. We plan to add pharmacy, dental, and vision services as the clinic grows.

Do I get special benefits for joining the waitlist?

Yes. Waitlist members get priority enrollment access and will be eligible for founding member pricing and benefits.

Will you accept insurance?

Onevia is membership-based, not insurance-based. You can use membership alongside a high-deductible insurance plan for major events.

Can't wait for Great Falls?

Join our Missoula clinic now. Your membership transfers when Great Falls opens.
