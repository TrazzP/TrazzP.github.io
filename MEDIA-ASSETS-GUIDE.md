## Technical Specifications

### Video Background Requirements

#### Resolution & Format
- **Target Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum
- **Maximum Width:** 1920px
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec) - best browser compatibility
- **Backup Format:** WebM (smaller file sizes)
- **Quality:** Minimum 720p; prefer 1080p for clarity

#### File Size & Optimization
- **Target File Size:** Under 10MB (strict)
- **Maximum File Size:** 20MB (absolute limit)
- **Compression Tool:** Handbrake (use "Web optimized" preset)
- **Lazy Loading:** Implement for files over 5MB
- **Mobile Fallback:** Static images (JPEG or WebP) for screens under 768px

#### Duration & Looping
- **Ideal Length:** 10-30 seconds
- **Why:** Shorter videos load faster and loop more naturally
- **Seamless Loop:** Ensure first and last frames transition smoothly
- **Content:** Avoid fast-moving content or jarring edits; prefer subtle movements, slow motion, or timelapses

#### HTML Attributes Required
```html
<video autoplay muted loop playsinline poster="fallback-image.jpg">
  <source src="hero-video.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <source src="hero-video.webm" type="video/webm">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>
```

**Essential Attributes:**
- `autoplay` - Starts video automatically
- `muted` - Required for autoplay to work in modern browsers
- `loop` - Creates continuous playback
- `playsinline` - Prevents fullscreen on iOS
- `poster` - Fallback image while loading

#### Audio
- **Always Muted:** Videos must include `muted` attribute
- **No Audio Track:** Remove audio during export to reduce file size
- **Controls:** Hide video controls (`controls` attribute not included)

#### Overlay Considerations
- **Darkening:** Apply 35% dark overlay by default for text readability
- **Alternative:** Use gradient overlays (dark at text areas)
- **Text Contrast:** Ensure WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 contrast ratio)

#### Performance Best Practices
- **Mobile Strategy:** Serve static images on screens below 768px width using CSS media queries
- **Preload:** Use `preload="metadata"` for faster initial load
- **CDN Delivery:** Host videos on CDN for faster global delivery
- **Testing:** Test on 3G connections to ensure acceptable load times

### Photography Requirements

#### Resolution
- **Homepage Hero:** 2400x1350px minimum (16:9 ratio)
- **Service Cards:** 1200x800px minimum (3:2 ratio)
- **Physician Profiles:** 800x800px minimum (1:1 ratio)
- **General Use:** Minimum 1920px wide

#### Format
- **Web Delivery:** WebP with JPEG fallback
- **Source Files:** Keep original high-res (4000px+) for future use
- **Compression:** Balance quality and file size (aim for under 300KB)

#### Quality Standards
- **Sharpness:** Crisp focus, professional quality
- **Lighting:** Natural or professional lighting, avoid harsh shadows
- **Composition:** Follow rule of thirds, negative space for text overlays
- **Model Releases:** Required for commercial use (covered by premium stock sites)

- ✅ Free for personal and commercial use
- ✅ Use on commercial websites, blogs, products
- ✅ No attribution required (but appreciated)
- ✅ Modify, edit, and adapt content
- ✅ Use in advertising and promotional materials

**Restrictions:**
- ❌ Cannot sell or distribute content on standalone basis (e.g., selling the video files themselves)
- ❌ Cannot create competing stock photo/video service
- ❌ No implied endorsement by people or brands in content

**Important Caveat:**
Pexels cannot guarantee every image has necessary model or property releases. Exercise caution when using images with identifiable people or private property for commercial purposes, especially in healthcare context.

**Recommendation:** For primary homepage hero and physician profiles, consider premium options with guaranteed model releases.

**Source:** [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)

### Pixabay License (Free)

**Permitted Uses:**
- ✅ Free for commercial and noncommercial use
- ✅ No attribution required
- ✅ Modify and adapt content
- ✅ Use across all media

**Restrictions:**
- ❌ Cannot sell or distribute content standalone
- ❌ Cannot use to create competing service
- ❌ No implied endorsement

**Important for Healthcare:**
Content featuring identifiable people must have an accompanying release giving consent for public usage. For commercial purposes (promoting medical services), you likely need consent or license.

**Recommendation:** Best for background content, landscapes, non-person-centric footage. Use premium for people-focused healthcare imagery.

**Source:** [Pixabay License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)

### Premium Stock Licenses (Artgrid, Storyblocks, iStock, etc.)

**Key Advantages:**
- ✅ **Model Releases:** Guaranteed model and property releases
- ✅ **Legal Protection:** Indemnification against copyright claims
- ✅ **Commercial Use:** Explicit permission for advertising and promotion
- ✅ **Healthcare Marketing:** Safe for use in medical service promotion
- ✅ **Broader Rights:** Fewer restrictions on usage

**Restrictions:**
- Varies by platform (read specific terms)
- Typically cannot resell or redistribute
- Attribution sometimes required (check terms)

**Recommendation for Onevia:**
- **Homepage Hero & Physician Profiles:** Use premium stock with model releases
- **Background/B-roll:** Free stock acceptable
- **Patient Testimonials/Faces:** Always use premium with releases or original photography

### Healthcare-Specific Considerations

**HIPAA Compliance:**
- Stock footage does not contain real patient information
- Real patient videos require explicit consent and HIPAA-compliant releases
- Never use actual patient videos without proper authorization

**Model Releases Critical For:**
- Images featuring identifiable healthcare providers
- Patients or actors portraying patients
- Any content used in promotional materials
- Images used to imply endorsement

**Safe Free Stock Uses:**
- Landscape/outdoor scenes (Montana themes)
- Non-person-centric wellness imagery
- Abstract medical/scientific visuals
- Equipment and facilities (no people)

## Placeholder Strategy

### During Development Phase

#### Video Placeholders
Use solid color backgrounds with text overlays while sourcing final videos:

```css
.hero-placeholder {
  background: linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%);
  /* Or healthcare-appropriate colors */
  background: linear-gradient(135deg, #4facfe 0%, #00f2fe 100%);
}
```

**Low-Res Placeholder Approach:**
1. Download target videos in lower quality
2. Use during development
3. Replace with optimized final versions at launch

**Blur Technique:**
```css
.video-loading {
  filter: blur(20px);
  transform: scale(1.1);
}
```

#### Photo Placeholders

**Services to Use:**
- **Unsplash Source:** `https://source.unsplash.com/1920x1080/?healthcare` (generates random healthcare images)
- **Placeholder Services:**
  - https://placehold.co/1920x1080/png (solid color placeholders)
  - https://picsum.photos/1920/1080 (random photography)


## Diversity & Representation Guidelines

### Why Diversity Matters for Onevia

**Inclusive Healthcare:**
Onevia serves a diverse Montana community and should reflect the full spectrum of patients who seek care. Diverse representation builds trust, welcomes all patients, and demonstrates commitment to equitable healthcare.

**Business Case:**
- Diverse imagery increases engagement across demographic groups
- Reflects Montana's growing diversity
- Attracts broader patient base
- Demonstrates cultural competence

### Representation Dimensions

#### Race & Ethnicity
**Target Mix (approximate):**
- 60-70% White/Caucasian (reflecting Montana demographics)
- 15-20% Hispanic/Latino
- 5-10% Native American/Indigenous (important for Montana)
- 5-10% Black/African American
- 5-10% Asian/Pacific Islander

**Approach:**
- Rotate featured hero images to showcase diversity
- Ensure homepage includes at least 2-3 different ethnicities
- If using stock physician profiles, show diverse medical team

**Avoid:**
- Tokenism (one image per ethnicity as checklist)
- Stereotypical scenarios
- Exclusively featuring one demographic

#### Age Representation
**Primary Target:** 30-65 years (working adults, families, pre-retirement)

**Secondary Target:** 65+ seniors

**Include:**
- Young families (30s with children)
- Middle-aged adults (40s-50s in fitness/wellness)
- Active seniors (60s-70s showing vitality)
- Multi-generational (grandparents with grandchildren)

**Balance:** Avoid skewing too young (doesn't reflect primary care) or too old (limits appeal to working families)

#### Gender & Identity
- **Balance:** Aim for roughly 50/50 male/female representation
- **Avoid Gender Stereotypes:**
  - Show male nurses and female doctors
  - Men in caregiving roles
  - Women in active fitness/leadership

- **LGBTQ+ Inclusion:**
  - Same-sex couples in family/wellness imagery
  - Gender-neutral wellness scenes
  - Search terms: "LGBTQ+ healthcare," "diverse families"

#### Body Diversity
**Inclusive Body Types:**
- Avoid exclusively showing athletic/thin bodies
- Include average body types and sizes
- Show realistic fitness (not just gym models)
- Senior imagery should show natural aging

**Search Terms:**
- "body positive fitness"
- "diverse body types"
- "realistic wellness"
- "inclusive exercise"

**Avoid:**
- "Perfect body" imagery (intimidating)
- Exclusively highlighting weight loss
- Unrealistic "before/after" implications

#### Ability & Accessibility
**Include:**
- People with visible disabilities (mobility aids, hearing aids, etc.)
- Adaptive sports/fitness
- Accessible healthcare settings

**Search Terms:**
- "inclusive fitness"
- "adaptive exercise"
- "disability wellness"
- "accessible healthcare"

**Representation:** Aim for 5-10% of imagery to include visible disabilities

#### Socioeconomic Diversity
**Authentic Representation:**
- Avoid exclusively aspirational/luxury wellness imagery
- Show accessible, realistic healthcare and wellness
- Middle-class family scenarios
- Community-based wellness

**Avoid:**
- Overly polished "country club" aesthetic
- Imagery that implies healthcare is only for affluent
- Exclusive focus on luxury wellness retreats

### Practical Implementation

#### Homepage Hero Rotation
Create 3-5 hero videos/images that rotate, showcasing:
1. Montana mountains (universal, location-based)
2. Diverse middle-aged woman in fitness activity

### Sourcing Tips for Diverse Content

**Best Platforms:**
- iStock: Strong diversity filters and collections
- Shutterstock: "Diversity and Inclusion Healthcare" collection (56,200+ images)
- Adobe Stock: "Medical Professionals Diverse" search (51,012+ images)

**Search Modifiers:**
- Add "diverse," "multicultural," "inclusive" to searches
- Use specific ethnicity keywords when needed
- Filter by age ranges
- Combine diversity dimensions: "diverse senior fitness"

**Quality Check:**
- Authentic representation (not staged/forced)
- Natural interactions and scenarios
- Professional quality without stereotyping
- Model-released for legal safety

### Avoid Common Pitfalls

❌ **Token Diversity:** One image per demographic group

✅ **Natural Integration:** Diversity woven throughout site

❌ **Stereotypes:** Limiting certain demographics to specific roles

✅ **Authentic Variety:** All demographics in varied scenarios

❌ **"Diversity Panel" Shots:** Groups awkwardly showing every ethnicity

✅ **Natural Groupings:** Realistic family/friend/colleague compositions

❌ **Exotic/Othering:** Highlighting difference rather than commonality

✅ **Shared Humanity:** Universal wellness and healthcare experiences

### Accessibility Beyond Imagery

**Alt Text Best Practices:**
- Describe key elements without over-emphasizing race/ethnicity
- Example: "Doctor consulting with patient about treatment plan" (not "Asian doctor with White patient")
- Mention diversity when relevant to content: "Multi-generational Hispanic family cooking together"

**Color Contrast:**
- Ensure text overlays meet WCAG AA standards
- Test with various skin tones in images

**Inclusive Language:**
- Pair diverse imagery with inclusive copy
- Use "they/them" when referring to generic patients/providers
