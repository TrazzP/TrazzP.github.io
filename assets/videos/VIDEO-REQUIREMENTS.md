## Video Optimization Tips

1. **File Size**: Keep under 10MB for fast loading
2. **Compression**: Use H.264 codec with moderate compression
3. **Frame Rate**: 30fps is optimal for web
4. **Color Grading**: Warm tones that match brand colors
5. **Motion**: Slow, steady movement works best for backgrounds
6. **Audio**: Not needed (videos play muted)
